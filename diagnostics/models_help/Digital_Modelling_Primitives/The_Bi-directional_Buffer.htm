The Bi-directional Buffer Model - BIBUFFER

The BIBUFFER primitive models the behaviour of a bi-directional tristate buffer. The model has two I/O data words, A and B. When the direction control input ATOB is asserted the A pins are seen as inputs and the B pins as outputs; when the control input is not asserted, the B pins are seen as inputs and the A pins as outputs.
