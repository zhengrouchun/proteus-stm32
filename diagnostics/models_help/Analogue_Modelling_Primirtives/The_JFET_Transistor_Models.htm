The JFET Transistor Models - NJFET, PJFET

The JFET model is derived from the FET model of Shichman and Hodges.

The JFET models have the following properties:

The dc characteristics are defined by the parameters VTO and BETA, which determine the variation of drain current with gate voltage, LAMBDA, which determines the output conductance, and IS, the saturation current of the two gate junctions. Two ohmic resistances, RD and RS, are included. Charge storage is modelled by non-linear depletion layer capacitances for both gate junctions which vary as the -1/2 power of junction voltage and are defined by the parameters CGS, CGD, and PB.

The parameters BETA, RD, RS CGS, CGD and IS are scaled by the AREA factor.
