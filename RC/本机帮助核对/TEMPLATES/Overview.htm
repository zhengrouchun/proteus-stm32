OVERVIEW

Proteus provides a complete and flexible scheme for customizing the appearance of your schematics. Everything from graphics styles, text styles, design colours, wire junction dot size and shape, etc. to a pre-configured sheet size, sheet border graphics, company logo and header block can be set up in a design template file.

Every design template file has an built in repository of graphics and text styles and also has a master sheet. When you initialize a schematic from a design template the graphics and text styles from the template will be applied in the schematic and the contents of the master sheet in the design template will be visible in every sheet in the schematic.

It follows that there are essentially two steps to creating or modifying a design template file: customizing the graphics and text appearance and customizing the master sheet with an appropriate sheet size, border, header, etc. These are discussed in detail in the topics on Master Sheet and Graphics and Text Styles.
